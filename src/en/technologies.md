A senior stack is not a list of tools. It is the ability to choose the right technology, operate it safely and measure the outcome. These are the technologies I use to build, integrate, observe and evolve digital products.

## Architecture criteria

Technology choices consider total cost, operational risk, security, observability, team maturity and future evolution. The goal is not to collect services, but to shorten delivery time without creating operational debt that harms the business.
