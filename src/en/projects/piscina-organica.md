# Piscina Orgânica

## Overview

Piscina Orgânica, by Sim Piscinas, turns landscapes from Bonito, Mato Grosso do Sul, into fluid-shaped pools. The website presents Mimoso 4, Azul 6 and Misteriosa 8, each with a real-place reference, dimensions and a path to a project conversation.

## Business challenge

Organic pools compete with straight-line catalogs people already know. Visitors need to feel the difference in contour, understand size and depth, and still reach sales without losing the contemplative tone of the brand.

## Solution and technical approach

The page balances landscape storytelling with product data: three models, geographic references, internal measurements, maximum depth and a WhatsApp CTA at the decision point. Inspiration, specification and conversion stay visually separated.

Each model needs its own URL, metadata and product data. Internal links should connect the Sim Orgânica line to Sim Piscinas without duplicating content. WebP, `srcset` and lazy loading protect LCP. Events `view_item`, `select_item`, `click_whatsapp` and `request_quote` must carry model, campaign and source into the CRM.
