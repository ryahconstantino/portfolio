# Yes Piscinas

## Overview

Yes Piscinas positions fiberglass pools as a leisure solution and long-term investment. The website brings together catalog, product lines, a 25-year warranty, quotes, customer support and reseller acquisition.

## Business challenge

The project needed to turn the broad promise of leisure into concrete actions: discover models, browse the catalog, request a quote or start a commercial partnership. Warranty and durability also needed to work as trust signals without hiding the buying process.

## Solution and technical approach

The delivery includes acquisition-focused hero sections, catalog and model navigation, warranty positioning, reseller flows, quote and contact CTAs, campaign-ready content and separation between institutional proof, product discovery and conversion.

Internal links should connect home, pools, catalog, quote and reseller pages. Each model needs unique metadata and structured data. UTM persistence through `view_catalog`, `view_product`, `request_quote`, `click_phone` and `reseller_lead` events allows consumer and partner funnels to be compared accurately.
