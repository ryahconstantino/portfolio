# Moura Multicar

## Overview

Moura Multicar is a car and motorcycle dealer in Caraguatatuba. The site combines vehicle inventory, brand filters, detail pages, vehicle appraisal and WhatsApp assistance.

## Business challenge

Inventory changes quickly and every vehicle competes for attention. A visitor should find a model, evaluate its price and condition, trust the dealer and contact a consultant in a few steps.

## Solution and technical approach

The solution includes car and motorcycle catalogs, brand search, indexable vehicle pages, detailed listings, buy-and-sell CTAs, test-drive flows, reviews, location and WhatsApp contact. A consistent vehicle model should cover brand, category, price, status, images and slug.

Vehicle status must be updated without leaving dead pages. `Vehicle` or `Product` structured data, breadcrumbs, Open Graph and stable image dimensions improve search and social distribution. Local SEO connects queries such as used cars in Caraguatatuba with address, phone, reviews and physical visits.
