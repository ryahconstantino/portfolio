# Vivite Saúde

## Overview

Vivite Saúde is a multidisciplinary clinic in Vila Lucy, Sorocaba, with more than 1,000 m², consultations, lab tests and imaging. The website presents 19+ specialties, faster scheduling, humanized care and a clear path to WhatsApp or phone.

The clinic is positioned as a complement to Brazil's public health system: nearby care in a few days, without claiming to replace SUS.

## Business challenge

A clinic with many specialties has to explain what it does, who it serves and how to book without turning the homepage into a bureaucratic menu. Visitors arrive with different intents: a faster general practitioner, a specific exam, a family specialty or simple trust in the facilities.

## Solution and technical approach

The delivery includes an appointment-focused hero, authority metrics, specialty navigation, lab and imaging highlights, team and technical lead, visible address and hours, and WhatsApp as the main conversion route.

Specialty and exam pages need unique metadata and `MedicalClinic` structured data consistent with Google Business Profile. WhatsApp and phone CTAs should keep UTM, specialty and exam type through to the front desk. `view_specialty`, `view_exam`, `click_whatsapp` and `generate_lead` make clinical, imaging and lab demand comparable.
