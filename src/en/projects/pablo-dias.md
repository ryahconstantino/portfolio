# Pablo Dias

## Overview

Pablo Dias's website is a personal brand hub: social profiles, a blog and a featured project, Método RS1. Instagram, YouTube, TikTok and articles about growth, leadership and operations sit in one destination instead of scattering the audience.

## Business challenge

A mentoring brand needs authority without looking like a static visiting card. Visitors arrive from social, an article or a referral and must quickly understand who Pablo is, what he teaches and which project deserves the next click.

## Solution and technical approach

The homepage works as a brand hub, not a single sales landing page. Social shortcuts, a blog on growth and leadership, a featured path to Método RS1, legal pages and cookie consent share the same identity.

Home, blog and featured project need their own titles, descriptions and Open Graph tags. `Person` and `BlogPosting` structured data help search engines read the brand. Events such as `click_social`, `view_article`, `select_featured_project` and `generate_lead` connect social traffic, reading and pre-registration.
