# Sim Piscinas

## Overview

Sim Piscinas is a national brand of fiberglass pools, compact models, premium lines, spas and hybrid solutions. The project presents Confort, Multinível, Praia, Retangular Premium, Retangular Slim, Spa and Chega Pronta lines, alongside accessories, quotes and reseller acquisition.

## Business challenge

The catalog serves different intent levels. Some visitors are comparing formats, while others already need dimensions, price and installation information. The experience had to organize a broad portfolio without losing focus on quote generation.

## Solution and technical approach

The delivery includes line and model architecture, product pages, premium positioning, separate B2C and B2B flows, warranty and reviews, phone and WhatsApp CTAs, and an administrative source for banners, lines, models and testimonials.

Line and product URLs support intent-based indexing. Breadcrumbs, product structured data and canonical URLs reduce ambiguity between models. WebP, `srcset`, explicit image dimensions and lazy loading protect LCP and CLS while preserving visual quality.
