# R & R Advocacia

## Overview

R & R Advocacia e Assessoria serves social security, labor, environmental, civil, criminal, consumer and healthcare matters. The firm has experienced attorneys, two locations in Caraguatatuba and an active legal content hub.

## Business challenge

Legal services require authority, clarity and trust. The site needed to present practice areas without excessive jargon, explain who handles each matter and route visitors to the correct location while respecting ethical communication.

## Solution and SEO

The project combines institutional content, attorney profiles, practice-area pages, blog content, locations, contextual WhatsApp messages and official-contact anti-fraud guidance. Each practice area should have unique title, description, heading and reviewed content.

For local search, truthful `LegalService`, `Organization`, `PostalAddress` and `sameAs` data should match the firm's Google Business Profile. Authorship, review date and references reinforce trust. Performance matters too: controlled scripts and optimized images protect LCP, CLS and INP.
