# Sim Conceito

## Overview

Sim Conceito sells customizable ceramic pools, accessories, tiles and reseller opportunities. The website presents models, a catalog, accessories, contact flows and a dedicated B2B reseller journey.

## Business challenge

A pool is a considered purchase. Visitors need to visualize a model, understand options, explore accessories and speak with sales. The catalog needed to work as a digital salesperson without becoming a cold product list.

## Solution

- Product pages for individual pool models.
- Clear separation between pools, accessories, catalog, contact and resellers.
- CTAs placed at high-intent moments, including quote and WhatsApp.
- Visual content connecting products to lifestyle and use.
- A separate B2B funnel for resellers.
- Product data, descriptive images and internal links prepared for SEO.

The catalog benefits from a single administrative source of truth. WebP images, `srcset`, explicit dimensions and lazy loading protect Core Web Vitals while preserving a premium visual experience.
