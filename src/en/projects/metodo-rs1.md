# Método RS1

## Overview

Método RS1 is mentoring and training for business owners who need to sell more, organize operations and sustain growth. The landing page speaks to pool-industry owners and managers, connecting sales, management, leadership, job-site execution and customer service.

## Business challenge

Mentoring pages fail when they attract generic curiosity. The page had to filter operators with a real business, explain the method without becoming a generic course, and turn interest into a pre-registration with revenue, challenges and timing.

## Solution and technical approach

The hero, top-of-page form, six mentoring fronts, pain-based segments, a three-step path and FAQ work together to qualify before the sales conversation. Average revenue is captured early so the team can recommend the right format.

UTM must survive from ad to CRM with form answers. Events `view_lp`, `start_form`, `generate_lead` and `qualified_lead` separate curiosity from opportunity. SEO covers mentoring for pool entrepreneurs without promising financial results the method cannot control. Accessible form errors and a fast hero protect mobile conversion.
