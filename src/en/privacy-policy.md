# Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 16, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Ryan Constantino collects, uses, stores, shares and protects information related to website visitors, leads, clients, business contacts and users who interact with digital channels, including WhatsApp Business, forms, scheduling links, analytics tools, advertising and automated support.

## 1. Overview

**Title:** Commitment to privacy, transparency and data security.

**Description:** We process personal data only for legitimate purposes, such as support, service delivery, business communication, experience improvement, campaign measurement, abuse prevention and compliance with legal or contractual obligations.

## 2. Data we may collect

**Title:** Information provided by you and technical browsing data.

**Description:** We may collect name, phone number, email address, company, role, messages sent, support preferences, project information, hiring information, interaction records, IP address, device identifiers, browser, pages accessed, visit source, conversion events and data from cookies or similar technologies.

We may also receive data when you interact with pages, ads, profiles, integrations, contact links, meeting calendars and channels connected to third-party platforms.

## 3. How we use data

**Title:** Support, service execution, personalization and analytics.

**Description:** We use data to respond to requests, prepare proposals, provide support, conduct meetings, perform contracts, develop websites and systems, improve content, understand user behavior, measure campaign performance and offer more relevant communications.

Data may also be used for security, fraud prevention, commercial recordkeeping, auditing, legal compliance and defense of rights.

## 4. WhatsApp Business and Meta Business Agent AI

**Title:** WhatsApp Business support with automation and artificial intelligence features.

**Description:** We may use WhatsApp Business and resources associated with Meta Business Agent AI to organize conversations, answer questions, qualify requests, route support, record contact history and improve user and client relationship efficiency.

By starting a WhatsApp conversation, you understand that messages, phone number, profile name, interaction time and other information voluntarily shared may be processed for support, request analysis and continuity of communication. Automated or AI-based resources may assist with responses, but relevant business decisions may be reviewed by a responsible person when necessary.

## 5. Meta Pixel, Google Tag and similar technologies

**Title:** Measurement of browsing, campaigns and support quality.

**Description:** We may use Meta Pixel, Google Tag, Google Analytics, conversion events, cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies to collect browsing and interaction data. These tools help us understand how users arrive at the website, which pages they access, which actions they take and how we can improve content, support, campaigns and services.

These technologies may collect online identifiers, IP address, browser information, device information, traffic source, visited pages, clicks, form events, contact button interactions and conversions. Data may be used for remarketing, ad measurement, audience creation, campaign optimization and user experience improvement.

You can manage cookies and permissions directly in your browser or through third-party platform privacy settings.

## 6. Legal bases

**Title:** Data processing according to legitimate purposes and applicable authorizations.

**Description:** Where applicable, we process data based on consent, contract performance, pre-contractual procedures, compliance with legal or regulatory obligations, regular exercise of rights, legitimate interest and protection against fraud or security incidents.

## 7. Data sharing

**Title:** Use of vendors, platforms and providers necessary for the service.

**Description:** We may share data with hosting, analytics, advertising, automation, CRM, communication, email, calendar, messaging, payment, technical support, storage, security and other providers necessary to operate the website and provide services.

We may also share information when required by law, competent authority order, protection of rights, fraud investigation, platform security or business reorganization.

## 8. Retention and security

**Title:** Storage for as long as necessary and protection proportional to risk.

**Description:** We keep data for as long as necessary to fulfill the purposes of this Policy, legal obligations, contractual periods, dispute prevention and commercial recordkeeping. We apply technical, organizational and administrative measures to protect data against unauthorized access, loss, alteration, improper disclosure or misuse.

No digital environment is completely risk-free. For this reason, we recommend that you avoid sending unnecessary sensitive data through forms, messages or public channels.

## 9. Data subject rights

**Title:** Access, correction, deletion, portability, objection and withdrawal.

**Description:** You may request confirmation of processing, access, correction, update, anonymization, blocking, deletion, portability, information about sharing, review of automated decisions, objection to processing and withdrawal of consent, according to applicable law.

To request data deletion, see the [User Data Deletion](/en/user-data-deletion) page.

## 10. International transfers

**Title:** Use of global platforms for operation, analytics and communication.

**Description:** Some providers may process or store data outside Brazil. When this occurs, we will adopt measures compatible with applicable law and the security standards of the platforms used.

## 11. Children and teenagers

**Title:** Services directed to professional and business audiences.

**Description:** The services and content on this website are not directed to children. If data from minors is improperly submitted, the legal guardian may request removal through the available contact channels.

## 12. Changes to this Policy

**Title:** Updates to reflect legal, technical or operational changes.

**Description:** We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. The current version will be published on this page with the last updated date.

## 13. Contact

**Title:** Channel for questions, requests and exercise of rights.

**Description:** For privacy, data protection or data deletion matters, contact us through the official WhatsApp listed on the website: [+55 12 99648-8381](https://wa.me/5512996488381).
